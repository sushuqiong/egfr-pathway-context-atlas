Dear Editors of Scientific Data,

We submit for consideration as a Data Descriptor the manuscript "Coverage-aware harmonized cross-disease transcriptome resource with expression-derived compartment scores" by Shuqiong Su (ORCID: 0009-0009-9217-6233) and Aiqun Liu (ORCID: 0000-0002-1077-5585).

The resource curates 26 public case-control bulk transcriptome cohorts across ten disease contexts (2,711 assay records, 1,086 patients with identifiers, 12 platforms), together with six tumour molecular contexts, four single-cell datasets and an archived analysis and quality-control pipeline. Its distinguishing feature is that comparability is quantified rather than assumed: every effect row carries the actual gene coverage of its module, cross-cohort direction consistency is reported and explained per context and module (including where it fails), and non-significant, not-estimable and not-testable results are retained in the shipped tables. The deposit contains the data tables, a machine-readable inventory with SHA-256 checksums, runnable reuse examples and the analysis code.

All data are public and de-identified; no ethical approval was required. The resource is deposited in Zenodo under CC-BY-4.0 and the code is available in a public repository tagged for this submission. The manuscript is not under consideration elsewhere, and both authors have approved the submission.
